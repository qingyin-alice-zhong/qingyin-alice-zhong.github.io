About Me

I am a Master of Engineering student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Cornell University (expected Dec 2026), with a B.S. from Southeast University.

My interests lie in microarchitecture design (pipeline front-end, branch prediction), hardware-software codesign, and FPGA prototyping. I have hands-on experience with gem5 cycle-accurate modeling, RTL design & verification (SystemVerilog/Verilog), and FPGA-based system development.

I am also experienced with AI accelerator microarchitecture (NPU/GPU), including dataflow optimization, kernel mapping, and on-chip memory hierarchy design for deep learning workloads.
